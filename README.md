## Research Questions Answered

| # | Question | Key Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Top 5 / Bottom 5 by voter turnout (2021 & 2026) | Bottom 5 in 2021 = all Chennai Metro (55–56%). Every one surged 26–30pp in 2026 |
| Q2 | Same party elected both elections — ranked by 2026 vote % | 71/234 retained. Edapadi tops at 57.97%. Every seat shows vote share compression |
| Q3 | Different parties — top 10 by vote % swing | Tirukkoyilur: DMK 57.15% → AIADMK 33.68% (−23.47pp). 163 seats flipped total |
| Q4 | Top 5 / Bottom 5 candidates by winning margin | Tiruppattur 2026 won by 1 vote. EPS top 5 in both years |
| Q5 | Party vote % split — regional level | TVK highest in Chennai Metro (46.63%). DMK lost 21pp there |
| Q6 | Party vote % split — state level | TVK 34.92% state share. DMK −13.5pp, AIADMK −12.1pp |
| Q7 | NOTA voting — which constituency voted most | NOTA fell 0.75%→0.41% statewide. TVK absorbed protest votes |
| Q8 | Postal votes % vs turnout % correlation | r = −0.016 in 2026 (near zero). No meaningful relationship |
| Q9 | District literacy % vs turnout % correlation | r = −0.72 in 2021 (strong negative). Weakened to −0.46 in 2026 as urban voters surged |

this analysis was prepared. The 2026 results are from the ECI live portal and
may differ marginally from final audited figures.
